# Humane tracker: Rustam's Music (web)
## 0.2 web (this release)
- New look from the Flagship family (light and dark), cardless Playing screen, album or blob hero.
- Companion: transparent outline that moves with the music and shows moods.
- Liveliness: six levels of liquid, spring-based motion; phone reduce-motion forces Still.
- Colours and accent can follow the cover (switchable). Warm at night. Visual silence after 5 s.
- Library: Continue, "Songs waiting for you" (veiled by age), albums, All songs, Search.
- Queue: blue edge and arrow for slipped-in songs; "Plays alone" chip.
## Limits (honest)
- Not gapless. Copies use phone storage. Background play needs Add to Home screen.
- The Companion listens through the browser's audio analyser. If music ever stops when the screen turns off, switch off "Companion moves with the music" (Settings) and tell me.
## Next ideas
ReplayGain, waveform scrubber, mood groups, swipe for volume, double-tap bookmark, lyrics, online music, "Updated. Tap to refresh" strip.

## 0.3
- Online lookups are asked for once, explained in plain words, and never run without consent. Nothing changes until the person applies, and Undo stays for 7 seconds; originals can always be restored.
- Suggestion card can be dismissed ("Not now" snoozes for 3 days) or switched off. No nagging.
- Visual effects respect Calm minimal, reduced motion and low power; colour is never the only signal (selected states use a tick or underline).

## 0.5
- The wake tap never changes the music: touching a quiet screen only brings the controls back.
- Insights show before they tell: filled stars, a 24-hour bar picture, a queue bar. Auto ratings are drawn lighter than yours, so nobody mistakes a guess for a choice.
- Ratings, suggestions and heat stay on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Lofi and DJ are off by default, start only when asked, and stop with one tap. DJ ends with the sleep timer.
- Limits: DJ and Lofi route sound through the browser's audio engine; if music stops with the screen off, turn off "Visuals move with the music" and tell me. Sections the DJ picks are a guess from your listening, and a new song has no history yet.

## 0.5.1
- Information changes by fading, never by sliding. Moving text is slow, leaves and returns gently, and stops for reduced motion.
- DJ spoken-audio check uses titles, folders and length, so it can be wrong. The switch in Settings overrides it.

## 0.6
- Mode atmospheres are calm and slow; grain and glow stop for reduced motion.
- Stretching very long files (over 12 minutes) is skipped to protect low-end phones; DJ skips songs over 15 minutes.
- Ambience was never tested by ear; it was checked by loudness and shape only.

## 0.6.1
- The frame-rate check runs once, 1.5 s after opening; it is not re-run if the phone slows later.

## 0.7
- Loudness evening needs the visualiser setting on (same sound path). A song is unlevelled until its first measurement ends (a few seconds); songs over 15 minutes are never measured.
- A-B repeat hides behind a long press to keep the screen calm. It is off in DJ mode.

## 0.8
- Sounds use real recordings at low levels; thunder is rare and softened. Nothing starts suddenly.
- Sleep ends with "Good night" and a pause, not a jolt.
- Pausing the music also fades the sounds underneath (same as before); with the screen off, playback relies on the browser keeping the page alive. This was not tested on a real phone.
- The sounds add about 4 MB to the app.

- 0.10: Library learns quietly (shelf order only), says so in one calm line, and keeps everything on the phone. Every hint says what, why and how in plain words. Charts show the week at a glance without scores or pressure.

- 0.12.5: one playing screen, no choice to make.

- 0.12.6: no hard seams over the lyrics.

- 0.13: what is playing and what comes next are one group; the controls are another; one quiet gap between them.
